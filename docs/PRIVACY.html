隐私政策

ma-bookmark（把书签做成文件树的新标签页扩展）非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明本扩展如何处理数据。

最后更新：2026-11-09

01

我们收集的数据

我们不会收集、传输或上传任何个人数据。

  • 本扩展完全在您的浏览器本地运行。
  • 您的所有书签组织数据（文件夹结构、折叠状态、排序等）仅保存在您当前设备浏览器的本地存储（storage 权限）中，不会发送到任何远程服务器。
  • 本扩展使用 favicon 权限读取网站图标以在书签树中展示，该图标由浏览器本地获取，不会被我们记录或上传。
02

第三方共享

我们不与任何第三方共享、出售或披露您的数据，因为本扩展根本不收集任何数据。

03

数据存储位置

所有数据均存储于您浏览器自身的扩展本地存储空间中，您可随时通过浏览器的扩展管理界面清除这些数据。

04

联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过项目仓库提交 Issue 与我们联系：
https://gitee.com/majingjing/ma-browser-bookmark/issues

English

Privacy Policy

ma-bookmark (a new-tab extension that turns your bookmarks into a file tree) takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the extension handles data.

Last updated: 2026-11-09

01

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or upload any personal data.

  • The extension runs entirely locally inside your browser.
  • All bookmark organization data (folder structure, collapsed state, ordering, etc.) is stored only in your current device's browser local storage (the storage permission) and is never sent to any remote server.
  • The extension uses the favicon permission to read website icons for display in the bookmark tree. These icons are retrieved locally by the browser and are neither recorded nor uploaded by us.
02

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party, because the extension does not collect any data in the first place.

03

Where Data Is Stored

All data is stored in your browser's own extension local storage. You can clear it at any time from your browser's extension management page.

04

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue in the project repository:
https://gitee.com/majingjing/ma-browser-bookmark/issues
